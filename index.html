LADDER RULES

How scoring works

+3Win
+2Draw
+1Loss

Three-row bonus. Play someone exactly three places above you and earn 6 for a win, 4 for a draw, or 2 for a loss. Your opponent earns regular points. Both players must already be ranked when the game is reported.

A fixed random draw orders players with equal points.
